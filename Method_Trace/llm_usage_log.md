# LLM Usage Log

## Tools Used

The following external tools were used during the project:
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Google Scholar
- SciPy Documentation
- Standard mechanism design textbooks and references

---

## Scope of LLM Assistance

ChatGPT was used for:
- report structuring,
- LaTeX formatting assistance,
- refinement of technical writing,
- debugging of LaTeX formatting issues,
- organization of submission folders,
- and improving clarity of mathematical explanations.

The core:
- mechanism formulation,
- optimization logic,
- objective functions,
- constraints,
- and implementation code

were independently developed and verified by the project authors.

---

## Verification Process

All equations, optimization logic, and implementation details generated or refined with LLM assistance were manually reviewed and validated against:
- the implemented Python code,
- generated optimization results,
- and mechanism kinematics.

Trajectory behavior and optimization outputs were verified through numerical evaluation and visualization.

---

## Limitations of LLM Usage

LLM-generated suggestions were not directly accepted without verification. Several responses required:
- correction of notation,
- clarification of mechanism terminology,
- and adjustment to match the actual implementation.

Final technical decisions and validation were performed manually by the authors.
